Photos

Search and filter

Plain words search what is in the pictures: cows, beach at sunset, birthday cake. The best matches come first; Date beside the bar puts the same matches in date order instead. Everything else below is a filter, and filters combine with words and with each other. Wrap a value with spaces in "double quotes".

What is in the bar is what you are looking at: the address carries it, so a search can be bookmarked, reloaded, and gone back from.

Where

in:placeThe town, region or country, as far as the location says. in:"New York".
near:lat,lon[,km]Within that distance of a point; one kilometre when the distance is left out.
bbox:w,s,e,nInside a box on the map. The map's Show in grid writes this for you.
is:located / is:unlocatedWith or without a location.

When

2024 or year:2024That year.
month:2024-07That month.
on:2024-07-22That day.
after:2024-07 / before:2024From the start of, or up to the end of, a year, month or day.
is:dated / is:undatedWhether anything in the file said when it was taken.

What

is:photo / is:videoStills, or videos.
is:liveLive Photos: the still with a moment of motion behind it.
is:screenshotScreenshots.
is:savedPictures an app or a browser saved rather than a camera took: downloads, forwarded pictures, memes.
is:editedPhotos edited in Photos. The original stays behind the edit, as on the phone; the info panel (i) opens it.
is:nsfwPossibly explicit, as the image classifier judges it, most certain first. sort:date puts them in date order.
camera:nameThe camera's make or model: camera:iphone.
file:namePart of the file name: file:IMG_12.
text:wordsWords written in the picture or shown in the video: signs, letters, receipts, screenshots. Every word, anywhere in it, in any case, a part of a word is enough: text:rechnung, text:"opening hours". The info panel (i) shows what was read.

From iCloud

is:favoriteFavorites: the heart set on the phone.
album:nameIn that album: album:Holidays, album:"Urlaub Polen mit Kindern".
is:whatsappIn the WhatsApp album: what WhatsApp saved to the phone. WhatsApp keeps no chat or sender in the file, so that is all there is to go on.
is:hiddenOnly the photos in the Hidden album, which are otherwise left out.
is:deletedOnly the photos in Recently Deleted, which are otherwise left out.

Like this one

similar:idPictures that look like that one, best first. s on a photo writes it.
face:idPictures with the same person in them, best first. Click a face in the info panel to write it.
sort:date / sort:scoreOrder word, similarity and face matches by date, or by how well they match.
